About Bus Bonanza

Bus Bonanza is a colourful parking puzzle. A long queue of passengers waits in colour groups, and a packed car park holds cars, vans and buses pointing every which way. Tap a vehicle and it drives out the way its arrow points; the right passengers climb aboard.

If another vehicle is in the way, it bumps and stays put, and parking spots are limited, so the order matters. Power-ups such as VIP, Arrange, Jumble and Undo help when you are stuck, and each level is scored with stars and coins.

3 quick tips

  1. Read the queue first. Look a few colours ahead, not just at the front.
  2. Look along each arrow. If another vehicle sits on that line, it will not move.
  3. Keep at least one parking spot free whenever you can, and save buses (10 passengers) for a long run of their colour.
